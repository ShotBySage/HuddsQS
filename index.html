About HQS

Everyone deserves a place to belong.

We recognise the diversity and strengths within Huddersfield and believe there are not enough affirming spaces for the LGBTQIA+ community.

The HQS committee dedicate their own time to creating safe spaces and finding inclusive places for our community to enjoy and thrive within.

Everyone under the LGBTQIA+ umbrella and our allies are welcome at HQS activities.
