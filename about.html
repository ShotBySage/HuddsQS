Who we are About HQS Hudds Queer Social is a community built around one simple idea: making it easier for LGBTQIA+ people in Huddersfield to find their people.

We want to create more opportunities for LGBTQIA+ people and allies to meet, make friends and feel part of something local.

From social meet-ups and creative activities to community events and collaborations, HQS is about creating spaces where people can spend time together without having to travel elsewhere to find their community.

Everyone under the LGBTQIA+ umbrella and our allies are welcome at HQS activities. We want our events to be welcoming, respectful and accessible to the people who attend them.

What we do

Make it easier to belong

Whether you're looking for friends, activities or simply somewhere to feel like yourself, HQS is here to help.

How it all started

A word from the founder

Kasey-Jaye Westbury (Kasee) She/Her · Founder

“I have always struggled socially and didn't have many options to make friends, so I wanted to create an opportunity for people in the same position as me.

Then there was a conversation between Arik, Kai, Charlie and I. We felt like we needed something similar to the LGBT groups in Leeds, like Bent Collective. It is difficult to make friends without being in uni. The idea was that if all the gays were in one place, we would be able to make friends a lot easier in our town.

09/07/25 was when the first message was sent in the group chat. Everything up until the committee forming was handled by me, aside from hosting. I made social media posts, hounded venues and stores to donate time or products to our cause, and had meetings with said businesses with the support of Ren n Charlie.

Then I realised this would get to a point where I wouldn't be able to do it by myself anymore.”
